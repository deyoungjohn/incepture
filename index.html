Principles

A few quiet rules that guide every tool published under Incepture.

  1. 01

    Real utility first

    Every project starts from a concrete, everyday friction—never a trend, a growth hack, or a solution looking for a problem.

  2. 02

    Small by design

    Focused scope, fast load times, and calm interfaces that do one job well and get out of your way.

  3. 03

    Built in the open

    Independent software written in free time, refined in the open, and shared on GitHub as each piece is ready.
